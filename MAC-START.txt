CLUSTER DESK BY N2WQ - macOS BETA

Extract the ZIP and move ClusterDesk.app to Applications. Open it and enter
your own callsign and cluster address. This is a beta and has not been
notarized by Apple. macOS may block its first launch; use the Privacy &
Security settings to review the app if you choose to open it.

Settings are saved under ~/Library/Application Support/ClusterDesk.
The Windows beta guide describes features and limitations; visual appearance
and system integration on macOS have not yet been fully verified.
